Snore Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 August 2026

What we record

Snore Tracker records audio only while a session is running. Recording starts after your chosen sleep delay and stops when you end the session.

Where recordings live

Audio stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly export or share a recording yourself.

What we store about you

Your account holds an email address and your session summaries — scores, durations, and the aids and factors you log. Summaries contain no audio.

Automatic deletion

Once stored audio reaches your storage limit, the oldest recordings are removed first. You can delete any recording at any time from Audio Storage.

Third parties

We do not sell your data and we do not share recordings with advertisers. Payment is handled by the App Store; we never see your card details.

Contact

Questions about your data can be sent to privacy@snoretracker.app. You can request full deletion at any time from Manage Account.
