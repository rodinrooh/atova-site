Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Atova, Inc. and Snore Tracker

This policy explains how Atova, Inc. ("Atova," "we," "us," or "our") handles personal information through our websites and our Snore Tracker mobile application (together, the "Services").

1. Scope

This policy applies when you visit atova.co, contact us, or use Snore Tracker. It does not apply to third-party services with their own privacy policies, including Apple's App Store and subscription services.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact and support information: your email address and the content of messages you send us.
  • Account information: Snore Tracker requires an account. You can create one with Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, or an email address and password. We receive your email address (or the identifier Apple or Google provides, which may be a private relay address), your name if the provider shares it, and a user ID assigned to your account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider; we cannot read them.

App information

  • Microphone and sleep audio: with your permission, Snore Tracker records audio during sessions you start so it can detect and analyze snoring and other sleep sounds.
  • Sleep-session results: session times, detected events, reports, trends, settings, and related app activity.
  • Setup answers: during setup you may tell the app your gender, birth year, sleep position, and whether you have nasal congestion, and you can log snoring aids and factors such as mouthguards or late meals. These help present your results. The app uses your birth year to confirm you meet the minimum age.
  • Subscription and purchase information: which plan you purchased, purchase and renewal dates, and subscription status. Apple processes the payment.
  • Advertising and attribution data: if you allow tracking when the app asks, your device advertising identifier, together with a small number of app events (such as viewing the paywall, starting checkout, completing a purchase, and detecting your first snoring night) used to measure our ads. See Section 4.
  • Diagnostics: Apple may share crash reports and performance data with us if you choose to share diagnostics with app developers. Snore Tracker does not include a separate crash-reporting service.

Where your sleep data is kept

Your sleep audio recordings, saved clips, night results, trends, setup answers and settings are stored on your device. The app does not upload them to our servers, and we do not send them to Meta, Google, RevenueCat or any other service provider. Deleting your account or the app removes them from your device. Because they live only on your device, we cannot recover them for you.

Website information

Our website provider may process basic server-log information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, and request timestamps to deliver, secure, and maintain the site.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain the Services; record and analyze sleep sounds at your direction; create reports and trends; remember settings; respond to support requests; diagnose bugs and improve reliability; protect the Services and users; comply with law; and provide notices about material service or policy changes. We use limited advertising and attribution data to measure how well our ads work. We never use sleep audio, and we never send sleep audio, for advertising.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose limited information:

  • to service providers that host, secure, analyze, or support the Services, under instructions and confidentiality obligations. These are Google (Firebase Authentication, which holds your account), RevenueCat (which manages subscription status, using your account user ID), and Meta (advertising measurement, described below);
  • when you direct us to share information or use a feature that requires sharing;
  • to comply with law, legal process, or a valid government request, or to protect rights, safety, and security;
  • in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Advertising and Meta. Snore Tracker includes Meta’s software development kit. If you allow tracking in the prompt Apple shows before the paywall, Meta can receive your device advertising identifier and the app events listed in Section 2, and RevenueCat sends Meta purchase and subscription events using identifiers provided by Meta. We use this to measure and improve our ads. If you decline tracking, the advertising identifier is not provided. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Snore Tracker. Meta’s use of the information is governed by its own privacy policy.

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscription management under its own terms and privacy policy. Atova does not receive your full payment-card number from Apple.

5. Permissions and your choices

  • Microphone: you can deny or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings. Recording features will not work without it.
  • Recordings and reports: you can manage stored recordings in Settings > Audio Storage. You can delete your account, and everything stored on your device, in Settings > Manage Account > Delete account. Deleting your account removes your account from our authentication provider, revokes the Sign in with Apple connection where applicable, and clears the app’s data on your device. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple account settings. You may also email us for help with a request.
  • Tracking: you can allow or refuse tracking when the app asks, and change it later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel an App Store subscription in your Apple account settings.
  • Communications: you may opt out of non-service messages using the instructions in the message.

6. Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including to provide requested features, maintain security and backups, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention periods vary by data type and how you use the Services. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it, delete your account, or delete the app. When you delete your account, your account record is removed from our authentication provider; subscription and purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed for accounting, fraud prevention, and legal obligations, and deletion may take a reasonable period to propagate through backups and systems.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Snore Tracker is intended for people 14 and older. The app asks for your birth year during setup and does not let anyone younger than 14 continue. The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you believe a child provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

9. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live and subject to legal exceptions, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, and to appeal certain decisions. Atova does not sell personal information for money. However, sharing identifiers and app events with Meta for advertising measurement (Section 4) may be considered “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising, or a “sale,” under some state laws. You can opt out by declining or turning off tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or by emailing team@atova.co. To submit a request, email team@atova.co. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You may use an authorized agent where permitted by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

10. International use

Atova is based in the United States. If you use the Services from another country, information may be processed in the United States and other places where our service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services change. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required by law.
